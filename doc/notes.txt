The extrinsic parameters of a camera describe its position and orientation in the 3D world, allowing you to transform points from one camera’s coordinate system into another or into the world coordinate system.
If two cameras are correctly calibrated, then a 3D point projected from each camera should end up in the same 3D position in the world. This is the fundamental idea behind stereo vision, structure-from-motion, and multi-camera calibration.

    / T2^-1 * T1 = I: sensors are calibrated, because point 
If both sensors are correctly calibrated, then their extrinsic parameters correctly describe their relative pose.
